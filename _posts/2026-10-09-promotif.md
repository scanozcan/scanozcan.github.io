[proMotif](https://github.com/scanozcan/proMotif) is a small R package that scans a gene's promoter for predicted **transcription-factor binding sites**. Given just a gene symbol and a TF (e.g. `run_binding_site_analysis("GENE NAME", "TF NAME")`), it retrieves the gene's transcripts from [Ensembl](https://www.ensembl.org), fetches the promoter window around each transcription start site (falling back to UCSC if Ensembl is unavailable), and scans both strands with the TF's position weight matrices from [JASPAR](https://jaspar.elixir.lu) and [HOCOMOCO](https://hocomoco12.autosome.org). Each hit is assigned a GC-aware match p-value, and the results are reported as a CSV table and plotted for the canonical promoter, per transcript (including alternative promoters), and on genomic coordinates. Human and mouse genes are supported, and an interactive Shiny app (`launch_app()`) lets you run scans and explore the plots without writing any code. proMotif reuses the Ensembl, JASPAR and HOCOMOCO fetchers of [GLproxScape](https://github.com/scanozcan/GLproxScape), and the first version is now available on GitHub.
